Malachi 1.0 – the first version.

Malachi is a private messenger for Android without a server: messages go straight from device to device, end-to-end and post-quantum encrypted, via Tor when needed. No account, no phone number, no contact list on our side.

Free:
- Chats, groups with pictures and voice messages, calls, and joining group calls
- Pair contacts via QR code or by matching phone numbers over Tor (the numbers are not stored anywhere)
- App PIN and fingerprint, Tor with bridges, disappearing messages, view-once media, Secret Chat and safety code
- Open and decrypt Seal messages from other messengers
- Copy messages safely (removed from the clipboard after 60 seconds)
- Available in 18 languages, including Arabic and Persian

Full version (one-time, no subscription):
- Emergency PIN, wipe after 5 wrong PINs and remote wipe
- Maximum anonymity: everything over Tor only
- Seal mode: send encrypted messages over WhatsApp and others
- Start group calls
- 21 design themes including a matching app icon
- Screen protection can be switched off, for example for your own screenshots (Secret Chat and Seal always stay protected)

Updates come signed directly in the app (over Tor) or from this website.
